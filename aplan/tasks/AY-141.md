## Why

`Deserializer::next` currently composes an owned `Value` and decodes via
`from_value`, losing per-node source spans on typed errors and allocating twice
as much peak heap as `next_borrowed`. In addition, bulk small-file workloads
(below the 4 KiB strategy switch) and late typed errors (which re-parse the full
document into a tree) have measurable per-call overhead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `Deserializer::next` decodes through the typed event path, preserving per-node source spans on Serde errors and lowering multi-document peak heap
- [ ] small-input setup overhead and streaming error-fallback costs are profiled and reduced where safe without changing error precedence or semantics
- [ ] regression tests verify identical `Ok` and `Err` behavior across `next`, `next_borrowed`, and `from_str`, with before-and-after instruction and allocation improvements recorded
