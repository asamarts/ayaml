## Why

Issue #178: every typed node held an optional owned or borrowed scalar string and a full
`OnceLock` for its cached hash, even when the scalar text was just a slice of the input.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] scalar text is an optional borrowed source slice
- [x] the cached hash is one atomic word with zero as the unset sentinel
- [x] a node is at most 80 bytes on 64-bit targets
- [x] the 11 MB typed peak falls to about 297 MB and total to about 420 MB; the 1 MB document to about 29.7 MB peak and 42.4 MB total; the 20 KB configuration to about 250 KB peak and 470 KB total
- [x] the short-scalar shapes fall again, with the remaining gap tracked by #178
- [x] allocation budgets are tightened and both test modes plus conformance pass
