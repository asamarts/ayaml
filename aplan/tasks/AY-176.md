## Why

Two bounded resource-hygiene improvements surfaced during the pre-1.0 audit are safe to defer
to `1.0.x` (they are bounded by `max_input_bytes` / `MAX_DEPTH_CAP` today) while remaining
worth fixing for production defense in depth:

1. **Eager pre-limit scalar and emitter allocations:** `Scanner::make` checks `max_scalar_bytes`
   only after `scan_double_quoted`, `scan_single_quoted`, and `scan_block_scalar` decode the
   full scalar, allowing temporary 2x to 3x allocations before rejecting inputs under tight
   `Limits::strict()`. Likewise, `double_quoted`, `expand_folded`, `TagDirectives::lines`, and
   `scalar_inline` allocate complete escaped/folded strings before `Output::push_str` checks
   `max_output_bytes`, and `event_emitter.rs` validates directives against `Limits::default()`
   rather than caller output limits.
2. **Native stack recursion in `ValueWriter` and `TagDirectives::scan_at`:** Unlike `Parser`
   and `EventWriter` (which use explicit heap stacks), `ValueWriter` and `TagDirectives::scan_at`
   recurse on the native thread stack up to `limits.max_depth`. Converting them to explicit
   heap stacks eliminates stack-size sensitivity when callers configure high custom depths.
3. **Minor redundant scan in `scanner/scalars.rs`:** `line_end` is computed twice identically
   inside the `leading < probe` loop.

## Scope

1. Pass `max_scalar_bytes` into `scan_double_quoted`, `scan_single_quoted`, and
   `scan_block_scalar` and fail immediately as soon as decoded length exceeds the limit.
2. Stream escaped and folded scalar emission directly through `Output` (checking
   `max_output_bytes` incrementally) and thread caller output limits into directive validation
   in `event_emitter.rs`.
3. Replace native recursion in `ValueWriter` (`emitter.rs`) and `TagDirectives::scan_at`
   (`emitter/tags.rs`) with explicit heap work stacks.
4. Remove the redundant `line_end` scan in `scanner/scalars.rs` and add unit/adversarial tests
   confirming tight-limit rejection with minimal peak heap and high-depth emission without
   stack overflow.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] oversized quoted/block scalars fail incrementally without allocating full decoded buffers beyond `max_scalar_bytes`
- [x] `ValueWriter` and `EventWriter` enforce `max_output_bytes` incrementally without full escaped temporary allocations
- [x] `ValueWriter` and `TagDirectives::scan_at` use explicit heap stacks and pass deep-nesting tests on small thread stacks
- [x] full workspace tests, allocation budgets, and Callgrind instruction gates pass cleanly

## Evidence

- Incremental `max_scalar_bytes` enforcement across `scan_single_quoted`, `scan_double_quoted` (including `\L`/`\P` expansion and trailing-space trimming), `scan_block_scalar`, `scan_quoted_line_break`, and multiline `scan_plain`, plus removal of the duplicate leading-blank `line_end` scan: verified by `incremental_scalar_limit_rejects_quoted_block_and_plain_scalars_with_exact_boundaries` and `oversized_scalars_fail_incrementally_without_allocating_decoded_buffers` (`in_child!` 8 MiB inputs under tight 64-byte scalar limits).
- Incremental `max_output_bytes` streaming for folded block scalars (`write_folded_body`), single/double-quoted scalars (`write_single_quoted`, `write_double_quoted` with 32-byte coalescing stack buffer), tag tokens/directives (`TagFormRef`, `write_tag`, `write_lines`, `token_char_len_capped`, `lines_byte_len`), implicit/flow key-width checks, and `EventWriter` directive validation threaded with `max_output_bytes`: verified by `streaming_folded_body_matches_expand_folded_byte_for_byte`, `value_writer_enforces_output_limit_incrementally_for_folded_and_tag_directives`, `event_writer_enforces_output_limit_incrementally_on_scalars_keys_tags_and_directives`, and `emitters_enforce_max_output_bytes_incrementally_without_temporary_allocations`.
- Iterative two-tier inline-plus-heap stacks (`TieredStack` in `emitter.rs`, extracted `emitter/value_writer.rs`, iterative `TagDirectives::scan` and `needs_block_value`): verified on 512 KiB thread stacks up to depth 512 in `iterative_value_writer_and_tag_scan_handle_deep_nesting_on_small_stack` and at `MAX_DEPTH_CAP` across all shapes in `value_emitter_handles_max_depth_cap_on_tiny_stack`.
- Performance and budgets: all 44 structured JSONL allocation baselines and all 31 allocation corpus budgets pass; all 63 steady-state Callgrind checks pass with zero exceptions (`emit` -1.31% to -13.06% Ir, `serialize` -0.50% to -5.89% Ir).
