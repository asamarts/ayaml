## Why

Each level of a key nested in keys hashes all that it holds to check for duplicates, so time grows
with depth times width. The depth limit bounds the factor at about 120, and the alias budget bounds
the width, but a 7 KiB document holding aliases takes 4 seconds on `from_str`, against about 0.1
seconds with one level of nesting, and a 3 MiB key takes 8 seconds against 0.35. `parse_value` takes
about half of that. It's been so since 0.1.0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] a wide key at the depth limit takes at most a small factor more than at depth 1, on `parse_value`, `from_str`, and compat, with a test that compares the two
- [ ] a duplicate at any level is still found, including one that differs only in the order of its entries
