## Why

`docs/limits.md` claims every check is tested just below, at, and above its limit.
Most limits are only tested far above, on one or two entry points, and several
reject-at-limit mutants survive.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] a table-driven test covers each limit below, at, and above on each entry point where it applies
- [ ] entries that cannot apply are stated as such in the table
- [ ] reject-at-limit mutants fail, with limit-disabling mutants excluded or run under a memory bound
- [ ] `docs/limits.md` describes what the table proves
