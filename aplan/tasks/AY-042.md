## Why

Only qualification branches use ayaml so far. The `from_value` change affects consumers'
config paths, and one consumer's cutover isn't tracked anywhere yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] the consumer requalifies against 0.2.0, including its `from_value` paths and its use of spans
- [x] its cutover has an owner and a task in its own tracker
- [x] the result is recorded

## Qualification record — 2026-11-20

Requalified against published `ayaml 0.2.2` (<https://github.com/asamarts/ayaml/releases/tag/v0.2.2>,
which supersedes the unpublished `0.2.0` tag and carries `0.2.1` plus `AY-122`):

- **Owner and tracker:** `asamarts`, tracked in `alint` issue `#481`
  (<https://github.com/asamarts/alint/issues/481>) with Phase 9 cutover PR `#605`
  (<https://github.com/asamarts/alint/pull/605>).
- **Compatibility, `from_value`, and spans:** `alint` upgraded its exact pin from `=0.1.2` to
  `=0.2.2`, adapted `stream_events` to `compat::parse_events_eager_with_limits` and its error
  decoder to byte-offset `Span::start()` with `Error::with_location`, retired all three depth-guard
  workarounds (`#139`, `#140`, `#141`), and removed all four defect entries from its differential
  allowlist (zero new deviations added).
- **Differential and parity verification:** 12 seeded differential long runs (3,848,472 inputs
  across value, JSON, `Config`, and `from_value` rule-decoding paths) passed with zero unexplained
  differences and zero panics; three 2,000,000-case runs of both depth-guard parity properties
  (12M cases) passed; workspace compare-mode tests, MSRV 1.88, public-API parser gate, and full
  preflight all passed.
- **Peak-heap comparison against `libyaml`:** `ayaml 0.2.2` stayed below `libyaml` on all nine
  benchmarked shapes (`0.517x` to `0.965x`, vs. the `1.2x` release bound; `21.8` bytes per input
  byte on OpenAPI-shaped YAML against `libyaml`'s `37.3`–`37.8`).
- **Second consumer:** also requalified against `ayaml 0.2.2`, adopting `compat::GlobalTags::Drop`
  and `from_str_seed` with all eleven local preflight gates (including coverage and evidence)
  passing and its upgrade pull request open.
