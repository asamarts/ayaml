## Why

A plain scalar's span and raw text include the whitespace before a comment, a line break, or a flow indicator. YAML ends a plain scalar at its last non-space character, and consumers that edit by span would delete the separation too.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] a plain scalar span and raw end at its last non-whitespace character in both modes
- [x] comments, line breaks, and flow separators remain outside the scalar
- [x] the value and existing event behavior stay unchanged
