## Why

The token arena grows by a fixed chunk until a sixteenth of its length is larger, which keeps
peak heap low. Each growth allocates the whole arena again, so a read of many short nodes
allocates many times its input in total. A 4 KB flow sequence of 1,000 two-letter scalars
allocates about 3.2 MB, most of it in about 30 arena growths of 13 KB to 220 KB, and a 32 KB one
about 27 MB. Aliases cost the same as the scalars they replace. `tools/alloc-corpus.tsv` budgets
peak heap and the number of allocations, not bytes, so nothing weighs this against the peak it
saves.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the allocation gate budgets the bytes each read allocates in total, as it budgets peak heap and the number of allocations
- [ ] on documents of many short nodes, a read allocates at most a stated multiple of its input in total, and its peak heap stays within the performance targets
- [ ] instruction counts on small documents don't rise
