## Why

A flow mapping key followed by blank lines before its `:` keeps a line break at the end of
its value. Releases since 0.1.0 do this, so it's a long-standing bug, not a 0.1.1 regression.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] a flow mapping key followed by blank lines before its `:` keeps no trailing line break, with and without an explicit `?`
- [x] block mapping keys keep their current, correct behavior
- [x] the changelog names the changed value
