## Why

The compatibility local-tag branch for non-handle `@` tags (added for AY-080) consumed every
non-blank character until whitespace or a flow closing delimiter. That accepted non-tag
characters such as `[`, `]`, `<`, and `>` inside the tag suffix outside flow collections, where
both YAML 1.2.2 and libyaml reject the tag.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] the non-handle compatibility local-tag branch consumes only tag characters (and `!`), refusing non-tag characters such as `[`, `]`, `<`, and `>` when not followed by whitespace or a flow separator
- [x] valid non-handle local tags such as `[?!@! x]`, `!@!%41 x`, and `[!@!]` still parse identically
