## Why

Issue #127: on mid-sized configuration files, the token arena's fixed-chunk growth copied the
whole arena several times, so total allocation stayed well above the live tree.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] mid-sized documents with ordinary lines reserve one token arena from the input length
- [x] long-line inputs and inputs outside 4 KiB to 32 KiB keep the growth strategy
- [x] the 20 KB configuration fixture's parse total falls from about 624 KB to 495 KB and its typed total from about 1.03 MB to 902 KB
- [x] peak heap is unchanged on the fixture, and the allocation budget is tightened
