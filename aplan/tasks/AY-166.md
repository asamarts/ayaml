## Why

A full audit of all 54 website and documentation files found a clear voice split between the
crisp, understated engineering docs (`docs/design.md`, `docs/limits.md`, `crates/ayaml-edit/README.md`)
and the newer site, benchmark, and migration pages, which rely heavily on em dashes (83 across
14 files) and recognizable AI-writing patterns: promotional slogans ("for production Rust",
"Benchmarks showcase", "Architectural transparency", "Optimization trajectory"), overstatements
("leak-proof", "Drop-in"), stacked negation templates ("no X, no Y, no Z, and no W"), filler
adverbs ("seamlessly", "cleanly", "naturally", "ergonomic", "actionable"), repetitive 6-item
taglines copied across 8+ locations, and generated em-dash fallbacks in `markdown-utils.mjs`
and `llms-full.txt.ts`.

## Scope

1. **Eliminate all em dashes across rendered site and docs content (83 occurrences across 14 files):**
   - Replace all 23 prose/UI em dashes (`site/src/pages/index.astro`, `site/src/pages/benchmarks.astro`,
     `docs/security.md`, `docs/migration.md`, `docs/getting-started.md`, `docs/limits-sizing.md`,
     `docs/migrating-from/yaml-rust2-and-saphyr.md`, `docs/migrating-from/serde-yml-and-forks.md`,
     `docs/error-codes.md`, `docs/decisions.md` D105) with natural punctuation (periods, commas,
     colons, or parentheses).
   - Replace the 4 `'—'` table empty-cell placeholders in `benchmarks.astro` with `'n/a'` (matching
     the rest of the file) and remove the 3 generated/comment em dashes in
     `site/scripts/markdown-utils.mjs`, `site/src/pages/llms-full.txt.ts`, and `site/src/lib/bench.ts`.
   - Convert structural `—` separators in `docs/decisions.md` (`## D<N>: ...`) and
     `docs/issue-priorities.md` (`AY-NNN: #NNN` or `AY-NNN (#NNN)`), updating the matching anchor
     link in `docs/stability.md` (`#d104-...`).
2. **Rewrite AI-sounding and promotional prose into direct, understated engineering copy:**
   - **Tier 1 (highest visibility):** `site/src/pages/index.astro` (deduplicate hero / KPI /
     guarantee cards, drop "Built for X, Y, and Z", "seamlessly", "leak-proof", "Drop-in", rhetorical
     question CTA), `site/src/pages/benchmarks.astro` (drop "Nothing is hand-typed", stacked
     "no X, no Y, ..." negation cascade, "Architectural transparency", "Optimization trajectory",
     "Transparent"), `docs/getting-started.md`, `docs/index.md`, `site/src/components/SiteFooter.astro`,
     `site/astro.config.mjs`, `site/src/pages/404.astro`, and `README.md`.
   - **Tier 2 (guides and migrations):** `docs/migrating-from/yaml-rust2-and-saphyr.md`,
     `docs/migrating-from/serde-yml-and-forks.md`, `docs/migrating-from/serde-yaml.md`,
     `docs/migration.md`, `docs/security.md`, `docs/limits-sizing.md`, `docs/editing.md`,
     `docs/SUMMARY.md`, and `docs/cookbook.md`.
   - **Tier 3 (reference and decisions):** `docs/decisions.md` (D105 and D106 prose),
     `docs/error-codes.md`, `docs/benchmarks.md`, `docs/tools.md`, `docs/api-reference.md`, and
     `site/src/pages/llms*.txt.ts`.
   - Standardize recurring labels ("Source-preserving editing" instead of "Surgical editing",
     "Benchmarks" instead of "Benchmarks showcase") and consistent numeric range punctuation.
3. **Add automated regression guard:**
   - Enforce zero em dashes (`—` / `&mdash;`) across `docs/**/*.md` and rendered site HTML /
     `llms*.txt` in `tools/check_docs.py` and `site/scripts/check-links.mjs`, with unit tests in
     `tools/test_docs_check.py` and `site/scripts/test-scripts.mjs`.
