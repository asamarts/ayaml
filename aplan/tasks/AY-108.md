## Why

A `Mapping` caches its structural hash, and every method that can change it clears the cache. A
`for` loop over `&mut Mapping` hands out mutable values too, and did not. A mapping changed that
way kept its old hash, so it compared equal to a fresh mapping and hashed differently. This is in
no release.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] iterating `&mut Mapping` clears the cached hash
- [x] the test of every mutation covers that loop
