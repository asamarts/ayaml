## Qualification status — 2026-11-14

Both consumer requalification pull requests are merged:

- alint `#510` merged as `f22ac682f94dba7f179199160407f3a625fa4cd5`; its hosted format, tests,
  clippy, MSRV, dependency, packaging, editor, and both performance gates pass.
- aplan `#38` merged as `7293c6bf2401b7879a0582ba933034e5902d971d`; hosted Actions are disabled
  in that private repository, so its full local workspace and pre-push validation are the evidence.

`ayaml 0.1.2` is published from tag `v0.1.2`. The property suite passes at 10,000 cases per
property, and the fuzz workflow passed on the tag commit:
<https://github.com/asamarts/ayaml/actions/runs/43555538837>.

The quiet-host performance run passed on `4c70a00`: every timing, allocation, peak-heap, and short-block
gate passed, including the compat typed paths, with the runner's load limits enforced. The gate
result is retained with the private comparison evidence.

The differential baseline, seeds, tests, corpus replay, consumer corpus, and evaluator jobs pass
against `4c70a00` after the comparison oracle was aligned with the deliberate `0.1.2` readings;
the corpus replay has no unexplained difference. A fresh differential fuzz window on the latest
oracle still has three open stops in malformed flow, anchor, and tag-handle inputs. An earlier
zero-open reading came from reusing an older window and is not evidence. The release remains
blocked on those fuzz stops; no behavior or limit will be weakened to turn them green.

## Comparison disposition — 2026-11-14

The corpus comparison is a review aid, not a boolean gate: it exits nonzero on any intentional
reading change. At `4c70a00` it compared 10,393 files. The changes were reviewed and are the
deliberate `0.1.2` behavior and diagnostic changes: 10,351 event streams, 100 compat results,
114 writer results, 28 document parses, 498 emitter outputs, and 28 value parses. The corpus
replay separately reports no unexplained difference.

That reviewed diff is accepted as the comparison evidence for this release under the review-only
disposition. `AY-005` remains blocked only on the three fuzz stops tracked outside this plan.
