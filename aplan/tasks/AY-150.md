## Why

A 1.0 release requires sustained soak testing across all fuzz targets plus final
verification of Miri, Kani, mutation testing, conformance, cross-platform CI,
allocation budgets, and instruction checks on the exact release candidate commit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] an extended multi-target fuzz soak (24+ core-hours across all fuzz targets under `-rss_limit_mb=2048`) completes with zero untriaged crashes, timeouts, or OOMs
- [ ] Miri, Kani, mutation testing, YAML Test Suite conformance, RFC 8259 JSON conformance, and all CI gates pass on the release candidate
- [ ] qualification results are archived and `M-v1-readiness` is ready to tag `1.0.0`
