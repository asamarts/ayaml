## Why

Ayaml retains the source span and immediate Serde expectation but drops outer
field and sequence context. Aplan consequently offers top-level blueprint
fields for a typo inside `tasks[0]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] owned, borrowed, seeded, root, and compatibility typed entry points retain nested field and index paths
- [ ] flatten, map, tuple, enum, option, and newtype failures have deterministic paths without duplicated prefixes
- [ ] the source span still identifies the offending token and `ErrorKind::Serde` remains intact
- [ ] the aplan blueprint fixture reports the nested task path and accepted task fields
- [ ] path bookkeeping costs nothing on the success path, and default `from_str` stays within its performance gate, which has under 1% of margin
