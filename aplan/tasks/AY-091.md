## Why

Issue #165: a compact sequence nested past the depth limit scanned the whole 32 MiB input before
reporting it, and `implicit_flow_colon` rescanned nested flow content at every enclosing level.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] a compact block sequence is refused when its depth first crosses `max_depth`
- [x] a nested flow collection is skipped in one step when looking for an implicit key's colon
- [x] the depth error reports the first over-limit token and its location
- [x] existing flow parsing and depth errors are unchanged
