## Why

Adopters migrating from YAML 1.1 parsers or building production configuration,
API-schema, CI, and linting tools need concise, tested guidance on YAML 1.2.2
differences, custom tags, multi-document streaming, Serde middleware wrappers,
source-span diagnostics, frontmatter, merge keys, and sizing `Limits` for large
documents.

- [ ] all Rust examples compile and pass through workspace `book_doctests`
