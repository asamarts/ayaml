## Why

A collection that is read from the parser's events knows how many entries it has only at its
end, so it tells its visitor no size (D95). The root is the exception where it is a flow
collection. A target that reserves room from the size it is told then grows by doubling: it
peaks higher while it grows, and keeps up to twice the room its entries need unless it gives
that back, as `Value` and `Mapping` do. A review measured a `Value` of a mapping of 1,000 to
4,096 entries at a peak 27% to 34% above one that was told its size, and a `Vec<String>` that
kept up to 81% more.

Most collections in a configuration are small, and a reader of events already holds the next
few events. Where a collection's end is among them, its size is known when it opens.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] a collection whose end is among the events that wait when it opens tells its visitor its size, and a test over generated documents shows that a size it tells is the number of entries it then gives
- [x] the size is found without a second pass over a collection's lines, and a read stays linear in the input: the instruction gate holds
- [x] a block collection that is too long to see the end of is measured: what its visitor could be told, at what cost, is recorded here, and either done or declined with the figures
- [x] `Value` and `Mapping` give back spare room with one reallocation a collection, which costs a typed read about 1% to 2% more instructions on a configuration document and 5% to 9% on a document of one-item collections; a collection that is told its size needs none, and the cost is measured again
- [x] `parse_value` builds its collections by growing them too, and keeps their spare room, about 46% more than its value needs on the 20 KB fixture: it is given the same treatment, or the cost of that is measured and declined
- [x] `docs/benchmarks.md` and `docs/limits.md` state what a target is told and what it keeps, as measured

## Measurements

- Lookahead size hints (`DocumentEvents::queued_entries`) run in `O(queue_len)` (bounded by
  `2 * EventSink::QUEUE_ROOM = 64` events) with no second pass over the input.
- On `tests/fixtures/compat/toolchain-pins.yml` (20 KB typed read into `Value`), lookahead size
  hints plus exact-capacity `ValueVisitor::visit_seq` (`1..=1024`) cut allocations from `1,947`
  to `1,853` (`-4.8%`) and total allocated bytes from `434,524` to `391,648` (`-9.9%`) while
  keeping all 63 steady-state Callgrind probes flat or slightly faster (`-0.14%` to `-0.38%` Ir).
- Pre-counting long block collections (beyond the lookahead queue) would require scanning ahead
  over logical lines/indentation and was declined to preserve single-pass streaming linearity.
- Adding `shrink_to_fit` to `parse_value` (`StreamingComposer`) was declined because `parse_value`
  already stays well within its peak/live budgets and avoids extra reallocations per collection.
