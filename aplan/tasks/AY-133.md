## Why

Streaming writers and pull cursors maintain multi-step internal state that can
fail on unusual call sequences or partial I/O. Deterministic seeded simulations
catch state-machine corruption cheaply and reproducibly in CI.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] deterministic fault-injection tests drive `to_writer`, `to_writer_value`, `to_writer_events`, and `to_writer_event_stream` with simulated short writes, `ErrorKind::Interrupted`, and I/O errors at every byte offset
- [x] deterministic state-machine tests drive `Deserializer` cursors through mixed `next`, `next_borrowed`, `next_seed`, error, and post-EOF calls across multi-document and empty streams
- [x] deterministic tests exercise `typed` streaming partial consumption, early visitor termination, and error-fallback behavior without panics or inconsistent state
