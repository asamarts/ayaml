## Why

Details of the exhaustion and wrong-value bugs were held back until a fix shipped.

## Release record

Published as [GHSA-f77x-cp9r-m4p3](https://github.com/asamarts/ayaml/security/advisories/GHSA-f77x-cp9r-m4p3)
after `0.1.2` was published and attested. The advisory covers affected versions `>= 0.1.0, < 0.1.2`
with `0.1.2` as the patched version. The changelog records the affected behavior and points to the
advisory and corrected limits. `0.1.0` and `0.1.1` remain published and unyanked: neither is
withdrawn, and the advisory directs users to the fixed release.
