## Why

During initial live validation of AY-135, the first draft of
`verify_inside_path_truncation_bounds_and_idempotence` passed a symbolic length near
`MAX_PATH_BYTES` (1 KiB) directly into `Error::inside` / `String::insert_str`, which
drove CBMC's CNF conversion (`converting SSA` / `Running propositional reduction`) to
26.1 GiB RSS on one thread before failing with `Out of memory` and threatening the
shared workstation cgroup. In addition, the Kani workflow ran bare `cargo kani` with no
address-space cap, peak-RSS budget, or per-harness timeout.

## Resolution and measured peaks (2026-11-22)

1. **Separated symbolic arithmetic from concrete heap-buffer transitions:**
   - Extracted pure helper `resolve_path_step` in `crates/ayaml/src/error.rs` and
     verified it symbolically across all `path_len: 0..=usize::MAX`, both separator
     modes, and representative step lengths (zero overflow, exact `MAX_PATH_BYTES`
     transition to `ELIDED`, saturating `u16` length).
   - Verified `Error::inside` directly in the same harness over the concrete boundary
     range `(MAX_PATH_BYTES - 4)..=MAX_PATH_BYTES` (plus `path_len == 0`) using a
     pre-sized small `String::with_capacity(48)` buffer so `String::insert_str` never
     takes a symbolic reallocation path, covering the exact `MAX_PATH_BYTES` elision
     transition, length saturation, and idempotence of subsequent `inside` calls.
   - Eliminated symbolic heap reallocation in `quoted_key` via stack-allocated
     `format_unicode_escape` and `push_quoted_key`, and concrete-looped `line in 0..=2`
     in `position::kani_proofs::verify_line_index_to_offset_bounds_and_surrogates`
     (dropping its peak RSS from 1,407.7 MiB to 341.9 MiB).
2. **Confirmed `verify_quoted_key_escapes_control_and_valid_utf8`:**
   - Re-verified directly through `cargo kani --harness verify_quoted_key_escapes_control_and_valid_utf8 --exact`:
     **PASS in 3.46s at 301.7 MiB peak RSS** (the earlier raw `cbmc --function` probe
     had hit an unwinding bound before `format_unicode_escape` replaced `format!`).
3. **Bounded sequential Kani runner (`tools/run_kani.py`):**
   - Runs every harness strictly sequentially in its own process group (`os.setsid()`)
     under a hard kernel `RLIMIT_AS` ceiling (`--harness-vmem-mib 3072`,
     `--codegen-vmem-mib 8192`), a per-harness wall-clock timeout (`--harness-timeout 120`
     with SIGTERM/SIGKILL to the whole process group and Kani's `--harness-timeout`), and
     a measured kernel peak-RSS (`ru_maxrss`) gate (`--max-rss-mib 2048`).
   - Wired into `.github/workflows/kani.yml`, covered by `RunKaniTests` in
     `tools/test_ci_checks.py`, and documented in `AGENTS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

### Measured per-harness results (`tools/run_kani.py`, Kani 0.67.0 / CBMC 6.8.0)

- `verify_inside_path_truncation_bounds_and_idempotence`: **PASS — 17.11 s, 457.9 MiB peak RSS**
  (down from 26.1 GiB OOM; well under the 2 GiB budget).
- `verify_quoted_key_escapes_control_and_valid_utf8`: **PASS — 3.46 s, 301.7 MiB peak RSS**.
- `verify_push_quoted_key_escapes_and_truncates`: **PASS — 24.42 s, 443.6 MiB peak RSS**.
- `verify_line_index_to_offset_bounds_and_surrogates`: **PASS — 4.41 s, 341.9 MiB peak RSS**.
- **Full suite (18 / 18 harnesses): PASS — 156.16 s total, 457.9 MiB maximum peak RSS.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `verify_inside_path_truncation_bounds_and_idempotence` verifies with CBMC peak RSS under 2 GiB at its declared unwind, or is replaced by an equivalent proof that does, and the measured peak is recorded in this task
- [x] the proof still covers the exact `MAX_PATH_BYTES` elision transition, path-length saturation, and idempotence of further `inside` calls
- [x] `cargo kani -p ayaml --all-features` completes inside `.github/workflows/kani.yml`'s 45-minute job on a hosted runner
- [x] the Kani step runs under an explicit memory cap (for example `ulimit -v` or `systemd-run --scope -p MemoryMax=`) and a per-harness timeout, so a runaway proof fails the step instead of the host or a shared cgroup
