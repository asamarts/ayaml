## Why

Even after `AY-173` shrinks `Value` to 32 B and `AY-179` eliminates eager `LineStarts`,
`Parser::parse_documents` still runs two-phase full-document buffering (`read_lines`): it
materializes every `Token` (32 B each) and `Line` (24 B each) for the entire document upfront
and keeps them alive until `StreamingComposer` or `StreamDeserializer` finishes building the
entire output.

DHAT profiling confirmed this transient parser storage accounts for **30% to 50% of peak heap**
on large documents (`2.09 MB` on `short-seq-20000`, `2.83 MB` on `short-map-20000`, `4.41 MB`
on `short-explicit-20000`, `5.6 MB` on `scale-map-1mb`, `9.5 MB` on `scale-openapi-1mb`, and
`~24 KiB` on `boundary-4kb-*`), and causes extra CPU cache misses from writing and re-reading
the entire token buffer before parsing begins.

## Scope

1. **Bounded on-demand line/token streaming (`parser.rs`, `parser/grammar.rs`, `parser/flow.rs`, `parser/pull.rs`):**
   - Replace upfront whole-document `read_lines` materialization with an on-demand bounded
     line/token window (`VecDeque` / compacting ring buffer of logical lines + tokens) that
     scans lines incrementally as the parser advances and reclaims/reuses consumed token
     storage as soon as lines are committed.
   - Preserve exact deferred-error ordering (`scanner_error` vs earlier parser errors such as
     `max_depth`, verified by existing limit-consistency and adversarial tests) and flow-block
     lookahead (`implicit_flow_colon` / explicit key lookahead) with zero semantic regressions.
   - Use compact `u32` token/line indices for inputs `<= 4 GiB` (keeping full `usize` fallback
     if needed) so active window entries remain cache-compact.
2. **Cryptographic `SipHash-1-3` hygiene (`mapping.rs`, `typed/stream.rs`):**
   - Retain cryptographic `SipHash-1-3` everywhere for `> 8`-key mappings and `> 8`-key `seen`
     sets (preserving full PRF-grade HashDoS resistance on untrusted YAML), while eliminating
     redundant per-parse `RandomState::new()` calls by reusing the process-wide seeded
     `GlobalHasher` consistently.
3. **Deterministic DHAT & Callgrind verification:**
   - Verify via `cargo xtask profile --tool dhat` and `memcount` that transient parser buffers
     stay `O(active lookahead + nesting)` instead of `O(document size)` on `scale-map-1mb`,
     `scale-seq-1mb`, `scale-openapi-1mb`, `short-*-20000`, and `openapi-spec`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] transient parser token and line memory at peak is bounded by active lookahead/nesting rather than full document size on block sequences, block mappings, and OpenAPI documents
- [x] `short-seq-20000`, `short-map-20000`, `scale-map-1mb`, `scale-seq-1mb`, `openapi-spec`, and `scale-openapi-1mb` show major peak-heap reductions (targeting `peak/live <= 1.35x` on large block docs) and lower Callgrind `Ir` / LL cache misses

## Evidence

- **Bounded streaming parser (`crates/ayaml/src/parser/window.rs`, `parser.rs`, `grammar.rs`, `flow.rs`, `pull.rs`, `cursor.rs`):**
  - Replaced whole-document upfront `read_lines` buffering across every parser entry point (`parse_value`, `parse_documents`, `parse_events`, `from_str*`, `SpannedTree::parse`, `Deserializer`, `DocumentEvents`, `EventCursor`) with `LineWindow<'a>`, scanning cache-friendly batches (`<= 128` logical lines / `<= 384` tokens, ~12 KiB) with lazy batch-boundary retirement (`retire_before` / `flush_retired`), relative-distance flow partner links (`delta = close - open`, invariant under both compaction and `line.advance(1)`), exact speculative-prefetch & deferred flow error ordering, and `O(depth)` compat parent tracking (`compat_parents`).
